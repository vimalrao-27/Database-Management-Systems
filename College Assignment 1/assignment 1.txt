As part of your DBMS assignment, you are required to select ONE real-world application or sector and identify the database tables that would be required to manage that application.

You may choose applications such as College Management, Hospital Management, Online Shopping, Banking, Hotel Management, Food Delivery, Library Management, Airline Reservation, Cab Booking, etc.

For your selected application, you must prepare the following:

Name and purpose of the application
List of all required tables
Columns/attributes for each table
Relationships between the tables
Brief explanation of the purpose of each table
Your work should demonstrate how a real-world application can be represented using a database.

Please make sure that the tables you identify are relevant to your selected application and avoid adding unnecessary tables just to increase the count. The database is supposed to solve a problem, not become a census of every object humanity has ever invented.
