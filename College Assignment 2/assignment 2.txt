ER Diagram Design and Relational Schema
Objective
Design a complete Entity Relationship (ER) Diagram for the same applicationdomain selected in the First DBMS Assignment and convert it into a Relational Schema.

Tasks
1. Identify Entities and Attributes
Identify the entities involved in your selected application.
List the attributes of each entity.
Identify the Primary Key (PK) for each entity.
2. Identify Relationships
Identify the relationships between the entities.
Determine the appropriate cardinality
One-to-One (11)
One-to-Many (1)
Many-to-Many (M)
3. Design the ER Diagram
Create a complete ER Diagram containing

Entities
Attributes
Primary Keys
Relationships
Cardinality
Use proper ER notation and ensure the diagram is neat and clearly labelled.

4. Convert into Relational Schema
Convert the ER Diagram into a Relational Schema.

Clearly identify

TablesRelations
Attributes
Primary Keys (PK)
Foreign Keys (FK)
For M relationships, create an appropriate intermediateassociative table.

5. Relationship Explanation
Briefly explain the relationships identified in your ER Diagram and their cardinalities.

Expected Output
Students must submit

List of entities and attributes
ER Diagram
Relationships and cardinalities
Relational Schema
Primary and Foreign Key identification
Brief relationship explanation
Submission Guidelines
Use MySQL Workbench, ERDPlus, Draw.io, or any suitable ER modelling tool.
The ER Diagram must be created digitally and should be clearly readable.
The Relational Schema must match the ER Diagram.
Upload the completed assignment to your GitHub repository before Saturday, 26 September 2026.
